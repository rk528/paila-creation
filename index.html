Approach

One clear step, then the next.

Every project is different. A typical engagement can move through four stages, so everyone knows what has been decided and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Start with the problem, the people involved, and what a good result looks like.

  2. 02

    Design

    Shape screens and flows before writing code, while decisions are still easy to change.

  3. 03

    Build

    Develop the website or application in clear stages, reviewing progress along the way.

  4. 04

    Refine and launch

    Test, adjust, and prepare the software to go live with the people who will use it.
